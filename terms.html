What Good Q is

Good Q reads Chinese calendrical charts — the four pillars, and three classical divination methods — and writes out what the tradition says about them. It is a cultural and reflective tool. It states tendencies, never verdicts, and shows the arithmetic behind each one.

Nothing here is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and it does not predict the future. Decisions about your health, your money or your legal position belong with a qualified professional. You must be 18 or older to subscribe.

Free and Plus

Free, with no sign-up: today’s elements and rhythm, the daily question, your basic chart and day master, and love and two-chart readings.

Good Q Plus adds the next twelve months, the life blueprint (ten-year cycles and years), the six topic deep dives, and the thirteen Tieban Shenshu methods.
