Privacy Policy

EnergyMap · 能量地图 — last updated 10 August 2026

🛡 Private by design — nothing leaves your device

The short version. EnergyMap creates your reading entirely on your own device. We do not ask you to create an account. Your birth date, time, and other inputs are used only to compute your chart on your phone, in the moment — they are never sent to us, never stored on our servers, and never sold or shared. We have no interest in your data; we built the app so we never have to hold it.

1. What we collect

Personal readings data — collected: none. The birth date, time of birth, sex, questions you type, and any second person's birth details you enter for a compatibility reading are processed locally on your device only. They are not transmitted to any server we control and are not retained by us.

On-device storage. The app may save small preferences on your own device using local storage — for example your light/dark theme choice and your daily check-in streak. This never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app/site data in your browser or device settings.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not require sign-up, login, or an account.
  • We do not run third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.
  • We do not build a profile of you across apps or websites.

3. Payments

If you purchase a subscription or a one-time item, the transaction is handled by Apple’s App Store under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We receive only the information Apple provides to complete and support your purchase (such as a purchase receipt). We never see your full payment card details.

4. Web hosting & logs

If you use EnergyMap through a web browser, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of request) for security and reliability, as is normal for any website. These logs are not linked to your readings or birth details and are not used to identify you.

5. External links

Some optional content may link out to our website or community (for example a blog article or a support community). Those destinations have their own privacy practices; this policy covers only the EnergyMap app itself.

6. Children

EnergyMap is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your control

Because nothing is stored on our side, you are always in control: closing or deleting the app, or clearing its local data, removes everything the app kept on your device. There is no server-side copy for us to delete.

8. Changes

If we update this policy, we will revise the date above and post the new version at this address. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email tarayoyo8@gmail.com.

隐私政策

能量地图 · EnergyMap — 最后更新 2026 年 7 月 10 日

🛡 隐私内建 — 数据不出你的设备

一句话版本。能量地图完全在你自己的设备上生成结果。我们不要求你注册账号。你的出生年月日、时辰及其它输入，只用于在你手机上、当下即时排盘计算——绝不上传给我们、绝不存储在我们的服务器、绝不出售或共享。我们对你的数据毫无兴趣；这款应用从设计上就让我们无需持有它。

1. 我们收集什么

命理相关数据——收集：无。你输入的出生日期、出生时辰、性别、你打字的问题、以及合盘时输入的第二人生辰，全部仅在你的设备本地处理，不会传输到我们控制的任何服务器，我们也不留存。

设备本地存储。应用可能会在你自己的设备上用本地存储保存少量偏好——例如你选的浅色/深色主题、每日打卡连续天数。这些从不离开你的设备，你随时可通过清除浏览器/设备中的应用数据来删除。

2. 我们不做的事

  • 不要求注册、登录或账号。
  • 不接入第三方广告或广告追踪 SDK。
  • 不向任何人出售、出租或共享你的个人信息。
  • 不跨应用或网站为你建立画像。

3. 支付

若你购买订阅或单次内容，交易由 Apple App Store 依据 Apple 自身条款与隐私政策处理。我们只接收 Apple 为完成与支持你的购买而提供的信息（如购买凭证），绝不会看到你完整的支付卡信息。

4. 网页托管与日志

若你通过网页浏览器使用能量地图，我们的托管服务商可能会像任何网站一样，自动记录标准技术日志（如 IP 地址、浏览器类型、请求时间）用于安全与稳定。这些日志不与你的排盘或生辰信息关联，也不用于识别你的身份。

5. 外部链接

部分可选内容可能会链接到我们的网站或社群（如博客文章或倾诉社群）。这些目的地有各自的隐私实践；本政策仅涵盖能量地图应用本身。

6. 未成年人

能量地图面向一般成年用户，不面向儿童。我们不会在知情的情况下收集儿童信息。

7. 你的掌控权

因为我们这边什么都不存，你始终掌握一切：关闭或删除应用、或清除其本地数据，就抹除了应用留在你设备上的全部内容。我们这边没有任何服务器副本需要删除。

8. 变更

若我们更新本政策，会修改上方日期并在此地址发布新版本。重大变更会在应用内提示。

9. 联系

隐私相关问题请邮件 tarayoyo8@gmail.com。
