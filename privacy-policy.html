Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

The short version: this app collects nothing. It contains no network code of any kind and cannot transmit anything anywhere. Every calculation runs on your device.

What you enter

The app asks for a date of birth, a time of birth and a sex. These are the mathematical inputs to a four-pillar chart and are used for nothing else. They are held in your device’s local storage, on your device only. We never receive them and cannot see them.

The optional profile

You may create a profile with a name, and optionally an email address, so that a chart can be labelled and more than one can be kept. This is stored in the same local storage on your own device. There is no server behind it, no account exists anywhere outside your phone, and no one else can read it. Deleting the profile in the app erases the stored record immediately and completely.

What you write

Anything you type into the app stays in local storage on your own device. It is never uploaded, published, shared, or made visible to anyone.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs, and no remote configuration. The app requests no device permissions: no location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, notifications, or App Tracking Transparency.

You can confirm all of this without taking our word for it: enable Airplane Mode and every feature of the app continues to work.

Deleting your data

Because your data never leaves your device, deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and no request you need to send us. To remove a profile without removing the app, use the delete control on the profile card.

Links away from the app

The app contains one link to the developer’s own website. Tapping it opens Safari. Nothing about you is passed along with it.

Children

This app is intended for adults. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is published on this page and ships inside the app.
