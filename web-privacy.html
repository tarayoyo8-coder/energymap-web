Website privacy

For goodq.app and the web app at goodq.app/app/. Last updated: 15 October 2026. The iPhone app has its own privacy policy. 中文版在下面

Short version: your chart, your birth details and your questions stay in your own browser. We do not run analytics, ads or trackers. If you subscribe, Stripe handles the payment.

What stays in your browser

The date, time and sex you enter, any optional profile name or email, the questions you ask and the readings are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device. The calculations run in your browser. We never receive this information. Clearing your browser data for goodq.app erases it.

Payments

When you choose a plan you go to a checkout page run by Stripe, Inc. Stripe collects your email, card details and billing address to take the payment and to run its customer portal, under Stripe’s privacy policy. Rootchakraholy Inc. receives from Stripe the email address, the plan, the payment status and the country, so we can answer support requests, issue refunds and send notices required for your subscription (for example a price change). We never receive your full card number. Your chart and questions are never sent to Stripe or to us.

After checkout your browser stores a Stripe checkout reference, so this device stays unlocked.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical request data such as IP address and browser type for security; we do not use those logs to track you.

What we do not do

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no selling or sharing of personal information.

Your choices

To delete what is stored in your browser, clear this site’s data. To ask about or delete the subscription details we hold, email taraachen@rootchakraholy.com.

Children

Good Q is intended for adults. Subscriptions are for people aged 18 or older.

Changes and contact

Updates are posted on this page with a new date. Questions: taraachen@rootchakraholy.com.

简单说：你的命盘、出生信息和你问的问题只存在你自己的浏览器里，我们不做统计、不放广告、不装追踪。订阅的话，付款由 Stripe 处理。

留在你浏览器里的

你填的出生日期、时间、性别，可选的档案名字或邮箱，你问的问题和得到的解读，都存在你这台设备浏览器的本地存储里，计算也在浏览器里完成。我们收不到这些信息。清除 goodq.app 的浏览器数据就会删掉。

付款

选方案后会进入 Stripe 的付款页面。Stripe 为了收款和提供「管理订阅」页面，会收集你的邮箱、卡片信息和账单地址，按 Stripe 的隐私政策处理。Rootchakraholy Inc. 从 Stripe 那里拿到邮箱、方案、付款状态和国家，用来回复客服、办理退款，以及发送订阅相关的通知（比如调价）。我们拿不到完整卡号。你的命盘和问题不会发给 Stripe，也不会发给我们。付款后，浏览器会记下一个 Stripe 付款编号，让这台设备保持解锁。

托管、我们不做的事、你的选择

网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，它可能出于安全记录 IP、浏览器类型等技术信息，我们不用这些来追踪你。我们不做统计、不放广告、不装追踪像素，也不出售或分享个人信息。要删除浏览器里的数据，清除本站数据即可；要查询或删除我们保存的订阅信息，请写信到 taraachen@rootchakraholy.com。Good Q 面向成年人，订阅需年满 18 岁。本说明更新会在本页公布新日期。
