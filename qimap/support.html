How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Qi Map Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

I paid but the care plan is still locked

Open any result page and tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Qi Map Plus box, using the same Apple ID.

How do I ask for a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your answers stay on your phone. See our Privacy Policy.
