Qi Map — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Rootchakraholy Inc
In short: Qi Map does not collect, store or share your personal data. Your answers stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

The symptoms you choose, your answers, and any tongue photo you add are kept only in the app on your device, for as long as the app is open or as you choose. We have no servers that receive them, and we cannot see them.

What we do not do

No accounts or sign-in. No advertising. No analytics or tracking. No selling or sharing of data. The app works offline.

Camera and photos

Qi Map asks for camera or photo access only if you choose to add a tongue photo. The photo is used on your phone only and is not uploaded.

Purchases

Subscriptions (Qi Map Plus) are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to that payment: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

Qi Map is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Health information

Qi Map offers traditional Chinese medicine wellness perspectives. It does not diagnose and does not replace a doctor. Please talk with a doctor before making medical decisions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions: taraachen@rootchakraholy.com
