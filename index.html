常见问题FAQ

你可能想问Things people ask

网页版和手机 App 有区别吗？Is the web version different from the phone app?

内容完全一样，同一份代码。网页版在 iPhone 上可以「添加到主屏幕」，之后像 App 一样一点就开，还能离线使用。Same content, same code. On iPhone you can "Add to Home Screen" and it opens like an app and works offline.

为什么一天只能问一次？Why only one question a day?

这是古法的规矩，也是准的来处：同一件事反复起课，卦就不再说话。明天 0 点刷新。It is the old rule, and where the accuracy comes from: cast the same matter over and over and the reading stops speaking. It resets at midnight.

它会预测我的未来吗？Does it predict my future?

不会，我们也不这样宣称。它给的是倾向、时机和一件今天能做的事。关于健康、金钱、法律的决定，请找专业人士。No, and we don't claim it does. It gives you a tendency, a timing window and one thing to do today. Decisions about your health, money or legal position belong with a qualified professional.

我的生辰会上传吗？Is my birth data uploaded anywhere?

不会。没有服务器，没有账号，没有联网代码。全部推算都在你自己的设备上完成。No. There is no server, no account and no network code. Everything is computed on your own device.

支持哪些语言？Which languages?

中文和英文，任何页面一键切换。英文版是给不懂八字的欧美读者写的，不堆术语。English and Chinese, switchable on any page. The English is written for readers who have never met BaZi — no jargon wall.
